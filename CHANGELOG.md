# Changelog

## 2026-09-19

**Guilds and characters**
- Delete an alt: a new "Delete Character" button in the header removes the character you're playing as (never your last one in a guild).
- Character names are no longer case sensitive when you join a guild: "Velvit" now finds "velvit".
- The Members tab now lists individual characters, each with its Discord account underneath, and officers can see it.
- Officers can remove a member's character (leaders can remove anyone's). Removing someone's last character removes them from the guild.
- Guild owners can change the guild PIN from the Members tab. New guilds use a 6-digit PIN.
- DKP: officers can remove former members from the table, and each row lists that account's characters. DKP is still one total per Discord account.

**Security and reliability**
- Tighter checks on who can view and change guild data, on joining with a PIN, and on saved builds and wishlists.
- Sensible limits on names, characters per guild and DKP adjustments.
- Extra browser security headers, and internal error details are no longer shown to users.

**Game data**
- September 2026 patch: new S1 armor and accessories, the bosses Grimturg and Exodus, and raised item level caps.
